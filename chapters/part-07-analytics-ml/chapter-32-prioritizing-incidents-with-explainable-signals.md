# Chapter 32: Prioritizing Incidents with Explainable Signals

> **Scaffold status:** Planned chapter; lesson and full lab are intentionally deferred.

## Learning objectives

- Construct priority features.
- Test rules against labeled incidents.
- Preserve human oversight.

## Measurable-outcome concept

Explainable decision support.

## Planned Harbor FCU scenario

In the entirely fictional Harbor FCU simulation, learners will prioritize a queue of fictional service alerts.

## Metrics to measure

- High-impact recall.
- Misprioritization rate.
- Review time.

## Planned executable exercise

Score incidents with a transparent rule and inspect errors.

## Expected takeaway

Automation should support, not obscure, accountable prioritization.
