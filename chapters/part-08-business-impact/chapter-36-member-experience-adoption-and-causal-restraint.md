# Chapter 36: Member Experience, Adoption, and Causal Restraint

> **Scaffold status:** Planned chapter; lesson and full lab are intentionally deferred.

## Learning objectives

- Define adoption cohorts.
- Distinguish availability, use, and sustained use.
- Avoid causal overreach.

## Measurable-outcome concept

Member outcome evidence.

## Planned Harbor FCU scenario

In the entirely fictional Harbor FCU simulation, learners will evaluate adoption of a fictional card-control self-service feature.

## Metrics to measure

- Eligible-member adoption.
- Repeat use.
- Assisted-contact rate.

## Planned executable exercise

Build cohort measures and list alternative explanations.

## Expected takeaway

Adoption is measurable; why it changed requires stronger evidence.
