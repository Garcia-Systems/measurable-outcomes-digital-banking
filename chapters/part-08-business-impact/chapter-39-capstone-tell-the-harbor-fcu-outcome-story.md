# Chapter 39: Capstone: Tell the Harbor FCU Outcome Story

> **Scaffold status:** Planned chapter; lesson and full lab are intentionally deferred.

## Learning objectives

- Synthesize evidence across the lab.
- Distinguish claims by evidence strength.
- Communicate to technical and nontechnical audiences.

## Measurable-outcome concept

End-to-end measurable-outcome storytelling.

## Planned Harbor FCU scenario

In the entirely fictional Harbor FCU simulation, learners will present the fictional Harbor FCU improvement journey across experience, integration, reliability, data, and delivery work.

## Metrics to measure

- Selected baseline/after metrics.
- Guardrails.
- Operational/member indicators.

## Planned executable exercise

Re-run selected measurements and deliver an output-metric-outcome-impact briefing, including interview-ready STAR evidence and limitations.

## Expected takeaway

Credible engineering stories show measured change, why it may matter, and what remains unknown.
