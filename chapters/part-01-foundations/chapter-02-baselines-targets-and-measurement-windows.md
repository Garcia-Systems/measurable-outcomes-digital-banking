- Choose representative baselines.
- Set justified targets.

Baseline and target selection.

- P50/p95 latency.
- Success rate.

## Planned executable exercise

Aggregate two windows and test how window choice changes the baseline.

## Expected takeaway

Targets only mean something relative to a trustworthy baseline and window.
