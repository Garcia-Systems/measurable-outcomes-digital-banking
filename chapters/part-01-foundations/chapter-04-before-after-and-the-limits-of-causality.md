# Chapter 4: Before, After, and the Limits of Causality

> **Scaffold status:** Planned chapter; lesson and full lab are intentionally deferred.

## Learning objectives

- Design before/after comparisons.
- Identify confounders.
- Distinguish correlation from causation.

## Measurable-outcome concept

Evidence strength and causal restraint.

## Planned Harbor FCU scenario

In the entirely fictional Harbor FCU simulation, learners will assess a latency improvement released during a low-traffic period.

## Metrics to measure

- P95 latency.
- Traffic mix.
- Completion rate.

## Planned executable exercise

Compare naive and segmented results and write a qualified outcome statement.

## Expected takeaway

A measured change can be real without proving downstream causation.
