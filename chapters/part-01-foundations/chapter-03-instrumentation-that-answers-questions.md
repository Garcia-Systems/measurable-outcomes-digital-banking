# Chapter 3: Instrumentation That Answers Questions

> **Scaffold status:** Planned chapter; lesson and full lab are intentionally deferred.

## Learning objectives

- Turn questions into events, logs, and traces.
- Avoid sensitive data collection.

## Measurable-outcome concept

Purposeful, privacy-aware instrumentation.

## Planned Harbor FCU scenario

In the entirely fictional Harbor FCU simulation, learners will instrument a fictional transfer workflow without recording account details.

## Metrics to measure

- Event coverage.
- Missing-field rate.
- Telemetry volume.

## Planned executable exercise

Validate an event schema against generated workflow events.

## Expected takeaway

Collect the minimum telemetry needed to answer a defined question.
